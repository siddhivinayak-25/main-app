# Veo Prompts — Product Demo Animation

Use one prompt per scene. Feed the corresponding screenshot and prompt to Veo, or use these prompts to animate a Ken-Burns-style sequence in any video editor.

---

## Scene 1: Homepage Hero
**Screenshot:** `01-homepage-hero.jpg`  
**Veo prompt:**
> Slow cinematic zoom-in on the center of a modern dark-purple landing page. The text "Agentic Hiring Intelligence" becomes crisp and readable. Subtle gradient light pulses behind the hero. Smooth, professional, no camera shake. 4K, clean UI, tech SaaS aesthetic.

---

## Scene 2: Recruiter Login
**Screenshot:** `02-recruiter-login.jpg`  
**Veo prompt:**
> Gentle dolly-in on a clean white login card floating over a soft abstract background. The card sharpens as it approaches. Subtle glow around the primary button. Smooth, premium product demo style.

---

## Scene 3: Command Center
**Screenshot:** `03-command-center.jpg`  
**Veo prompt:**
> Slow pan from left to right across a recruiter dashboard with stat cards and charts. Focus lands on the pipeline graph and the top-candidates list. Maintain crisp UI readability. Smooth motion, no blur.

## Scene 4: Tests List
**Screenshot:** `04-tests-list.jpg`  
**Veo prompt:**
> Steady upward scroll motion through a table of assessment tests. One active row subtly highlights. Clean, modern dashboard aesthetic. Text stays sharp and legible throughout the move.

## Scene 5: Create Test

## Scene 6: Pipeline

## Master prompt for Veo (if you want one combined generation)
> A 60-second professional SaaS product demo video. Start with a dark purple landing page, move through a recruiter login, a dashboard, a test creation form, an invitations table, a candidate welcome page, a browser IDE with an AI panel, then back to a recruiter dashboard and a session replay screen. Slow smooth camera moves: zooms, pans, and tracking shots. Crisp UI text, modern gradient colors, no camera shake, premium tech aesthetic. 4K.
